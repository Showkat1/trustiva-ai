TITLE: Cyber Crime - Risks, Prevention and Legal Remedies

Electronic financial fraud can occur through online means such as email, websites and social media.

Fraudsters may impersonate authorized representatives of banks, government programs, cellular companies or other organizations.

Fraudsters may attempt to obtain confidential information such as account information, PINs, dates of birth and One-Time Passwords (OTPs).

Fraudsters may use prize-winning schemes and impersonation of game shows to deceive victims.

Fraudsters may create false online profiles and build trust before requesting money for purposes such as tickets, customs fees, visa fees or investments.

Users should treat unexpected requests for confidential financial or authentication information with caution.

Suspicious claims involving financial rewards, prizes, government programs, investments or requests for money should be independently verified before taking action.
