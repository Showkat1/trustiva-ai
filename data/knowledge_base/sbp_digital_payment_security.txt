TITLE: Security of Digital Payments

Digital payment systems require security controls and customer awareness.

Banks and Microfinance Banks are required to provide transaction alerts for digital transactions.

Customers should pay attention to transaction notifications and digital banking security.

Banks are also expected to educate customers about online banking fraud and preventive measures.

Digital banking fraud can involve social engineering and fake calls.

Suspicious communications involving financial transactions should be treated carefully and independently verified.

Customers should avoid providing sensitive authentication information to unverified parties.
