TITLE: Job and Investment Scam Indicators

Potential job scams may include:

- Unsolicited job offers
- Unrealistically high salaries
- Requests for registration fees
- Requests for training fees before employment
- Requests for identity documents
- Requests for banking credentials
- Requests for OTPs
- Pressure to pay quickly

Potential investment scams may include:

- Guaranteed profits
- Extremely high returns
- Pressure to invest immediately
- Requests for cryptocurrency or bank transfers
- Requests for advance fees
- Referral-based financial promises
- Claims that losses are impossible

Users should independently verify employers, investment companies, domains and payment requests before sending money or confidential information.
